Booking a consultation

You can book a consultation by email, by phone, on WhatsApp, or by using the enquiry form offered by the assistant on this website.

An initial consultation lasts up to 45 minutes and costs 50,000 naira. It can take place at either office or by video call. If you instruct the firm on the matter afterwards, the consultation fee is deducted from your first invoice.

What happens after you contact us

We reply to every enquiry within one working day. Before we discuss the details of your matter, we run a conflict check to make sure we are not already acting for someone on the other side. After the conflict check, a lawyer from the relevant practice area will contact you.

Urgent matters

If a court date, a deadline or an eviction is less than seven days away, please say so in your first message, or call the Lagos office directly so that we can prioritise it.
