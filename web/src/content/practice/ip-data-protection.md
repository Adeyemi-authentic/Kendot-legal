We help businesses protect their brands and handle personal data lawfully.

## Intellectual property

- **Trademarks:** we run availability searches, file applications at the Trademarks Registry, respond to objections, handle oppositions, and renew registrations.
- **Copyright and licensing:** we draft licence, assignment and content agreements for creative and software businesses.
- **Enforcement:** we send cease-and-desist letters and act in infringement and passing-off claims.

## Data protection

- **Compliance programmes:** we map the personal data a business holds, draft privacy notices and policies, and prepare data processing agreements with vendors.
- **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023:** we advise on lawful bases for processing, data subject rights, cross-border transfers and whether a business is a data controller or processor of major importance.
- **Breaches:** we help clients respond to personal data breaches, including notifying the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
- **AI and data:** we advise businesses adopting AI tools on confidentiality, data protection and supplier terms.
