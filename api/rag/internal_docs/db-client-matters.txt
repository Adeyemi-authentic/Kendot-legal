How to read these records

Each matter has a Matter ID (KL-2026-001 to KL-2026-012). These are matter numbers, not website enquiry references (those look like KL-0001). The Lead Lawyer is primarily responsible for the matter. A Supporting Lawyer assists but is not the lead. A Supervising Partner has oversight only where a record says so. A Senior Associate role on a matter is stated where it applies.

Priority levels are Critical, High and Medium. A Critical matter needs senior-level attention. An action that an internal note says needs a partner's approval has not been done unless the record says so. A record that does not mention litigation steps means the client has not instructed the firm to litigate.
