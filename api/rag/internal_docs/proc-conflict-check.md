New Matter Intake and Conflict Check Procedure

Reference: KL/INT/PROC-005. Owner: Halima Sani (Partner, Risk and Compliance lead). Last reviewed: April 2026.
Internal document of Kendot Legal, a fictional firm. Demonstration content only. Not for clients.

Why this matters

We cannot act for a client against a current client, or against a former client on a related matter, without the right consents. A missed conflict can force us to stop acting mid-matter. Every new matter goes through this procedure, including small ones and website enquiries.

Step 1: Record the enquiry

Website enquiries arrive in the enquiries list with a reference starting with KL-. The front desk logs every enquiry in the matter register on the same working day, with the reference.

Step 2: Run the conflict search

Search the client database for:
1. The prospective client (and, for a company, its directors and major shareholders).
2. Every other party named in the enquiry, including the opponent and their lawyers where known.
3. Related companies in the same group.

Step 3: Decide

- No matches: the associate records "clear" on the matter register.
- A match with a current client on the other side: STOP. Escalate to Halima Sani the same day. Do not contact the prospective client about the substance of the matter.
- A match with a former client: the supervising partner decides whether the matters are related and records the reason.

Step 4: Know-your-client checks

For a company, get the CAC search and proof of the directors' identity. For an individual, get a government ID. Matters involving large property or share transactions also need proof of the source of funds.

Step 5: Open the matter

Only after Steps 1 to 4 are complete: open the matter number, send the engagement letter (KL/INT/TMPL-004), and set up the file.

Timing

Aim to complete the conflict check within one working day, so that we can still meet the promise on the website to reply to enquiries within one working day.
