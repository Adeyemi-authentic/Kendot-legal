Kendot Legal is a commercial law firm with offices in Lagos and Abuja. The firm was founded in 2012 and advises businesses, investors, landlords, tenants and individuals.

The firm has five practice areas: Corporate and Commercial, Real Estate and Tenancy, Tax, Intellectual Property and Data Protection, and Dispute Resolution.

## Our approach

We explain the law in plain language, give a clear estimate of cost before work starts, and tell clients early when a matter is better settled than litigated. Every new matter is supervised by a partner.

## Who we act for

Our clients include small and medium-sized businesses, start-ups, property owners and managers, foreign investors setting up in Nigeria, and individuals with property, business or rights-related disputes.

## What we do not handle

Kendot Legal does not handle criminal defence, family law (divorce, custody or adoption), or immigration applications. If your matter falls in one of these areas, we are happy to suggest that you contact the Nigerian Bar Association branch in your area for a referral.
