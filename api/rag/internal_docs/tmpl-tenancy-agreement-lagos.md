Standard Yearly Tenancy Agreement (Lagos): Template Notes

Reference: KL/INT/TMPL-002. Owner: Tomi Kendot (Managing Partner, Real Estate and Tenancy). Last reviewed: January 2026.
Internal document of Kendot Legal, a fictional firm. Demonstration content only. Not for clients.

When to use this template

Use this template for residential and small commercial lettings in Lagos State with a yearly term. For premises outside Lagos, check the local tenancy law with the Real Estate team first, because the Lagos rules on notice and advance rent do not apply there. For shopping malls or multi-tenant office blocks, use the commercial lease precedent instead.

Key clauses and the firm's positions

- Term and renewal (clause 2): a fixed one-year term. Renewal only by written agreement. No automatic renewal.
- Rent and advance rent (clause 3): under the Lagos Tenancy Law 2011, a landlord may not demand more than one year's rent in advance from a new yearly tenant. The template reflects this. If a landlord client asks for two years in advance, explain the rule and record the advice on the file.
- Service charge (clause 4): set out separately from rent, with a list of what it covers.
- Security deposit (clause 5): refundable, held for repairs and unpaid bills, and returned within 30 days after the tenant leaves, less documented deductions.
- Notice to quit (clause 11): for a yearly tenancy, six months' written notice, as the Lagos Tenancy Law requires. This matches the firm's public insight on notice to quit.
- Repairs (clause 7): structural repairs for the landlord, internal and decorative repairs for the tenant.
- Dispute resolution (clause 14): mediation first (Tomi Kendot is a trained mediator, but must not mediate a dispute for a firm client), then the courts.

Checklist before sending the draft to a client

1. Confirm the landlord's title documents are on file (Certificate of Occupancy or Governor's Consent).
2. Fill in the rent receipt wording in Schedule 2. Landlords must issue receipts for rent paid.
3. Delete the commercial-use clause for residential lets.
4. Ask Bayo Adewale (Associate) to proofread the schedules. Most errors in past matters were in the schedules, not the main clauses.
