Client Data Breach Response Playbook (NDPA)

Reference: KL/INT/PROC-003. Owner: Chidera Eze (Senior Associate, IP and Data Protection). Supervising partner: Ifeanyi Obi. Last reviewed: May 2026.
Internal document of Kendot Legal, a fictional firm. Demonstration content only. Not for clients.

Purpose

This playbook is for when a CLIENT calls us about a personal data breach. For a breach of the firm's own systems, follow the firm's internal IT incident policy instead and tell the Managing Partner at once.

The deadline that drives everything

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, a data controller must notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach that is likely to put people's rights and freedoms at risk. The clock starts when the client becomes aware, not when they call us. Ask for that time in the first call and write it on the file.

First call: what to ask (use the intake form in Annex A)

1. When did the client first become aware of the breach?
2. What happened, and is it still happening?
3. What kinds of personal data are involved, and roughly how many people?
4. Is any sensitive data involved (health, financial, children's data)?
5. Has anyone outside the client been told yet (police, the press, customers)?

Within the first 24 hours

- Open a matter and run the conflict check (KL/INT/PROC-005).
- Chidera, or the duty associate, prepares a first risk assessment: low, medium or high risk to the people affected.
- Draft the NDPC notification using the template in Annex B, even if the facts are incomplete. It can be updated later.
- If the risk to people is high, draft the notice to the people affected (Annex C) in plain English.

Before the 72 hours run out

- The supervising partner approves the notification.
- The client files the notification, or we file it for them with written instructions.
- Record the filing time and keep a copy on the file.

After the deadline

- Help the client keep a breach log entry, as the Act expects.
- Book a lessons-learned call within 2 weeks and offer a review of the client's breach response plan (a separate fee).
